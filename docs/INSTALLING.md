# Installing FDP Client

Open the website's download page and choose the build for your setup. The cards show build availability and a SHA-256 checksum when the build service provides one. [Downloads](https://fdpinfo.github.io/download/).

## Forge

Use the main FDP Client card for the Forge build. The **Forge** steps are under **How to install** on the download page. [Forge download and setup](https://fdpinfo.github.io/download/).

## FDP Client Inject with ZAVZ

Get the Inject JAR from its own card and the ZAVZ executor from the official download page. Select **Install with ZAVZ** on the card for the walkthrough, then follow the current build guide for supported clients and versions.

- [FDP Client Inject build](https://fdpinfo.github.io/download/#inject)
- [Install with ZAVZ](https://fdpinfo.github.io/download/#install-inject)
- [ZAVZ downloads](https://lucas-lima.xyz/archives/zavz-inject)
- [FDPClient build guide](https://github.com/SkidderMC/FDPClient/blob/main/docs/LUNAR.md)
